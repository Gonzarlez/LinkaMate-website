Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

LinkaMate respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use LinkaMate products, applications, websites and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LinkaMate, we may collect information including:

  • Account information, such as usernames and account identifiers.
  • Information you provide when using LinkaMate services.
  • Friend connections and interactions within LinkaMate products.
  • Device and technical information required to operate our services.
  • Usage information relating to how our applications and services are used.
  • Information you provide when contacting us for support or enquiries.

2. TapMate

TapMate is a LinkaMate product designed to allow users to send simple interactions, or "taps", between supported devices.

To provide this functionality, TapMate may process information such as usernames, friend connections, sender and recipient identifiers, selected tap types, timestamps and technical information required to transmit and deliver taps.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate LinkaMate services.
  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Enable interactions between users.
  • Deliver TapMate taps and related functionality.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve our services.
  • Respond to support requests and enquiries.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to host, operate, secure or maintain LinkaMate services. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the rights, security or integrity of LinkaMate, its users or others.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by LinkaMate. However, no internet-connected service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet operational requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Third-Party Services

LinkaMate may rely on third-party infrastructure and services to provide features such as hosting, databases, communications and application distribution. Those providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services.

8. Children's Privacy

LinkaMate services are not intended for children where use would require parental consent under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may contact us using the details below regarding a privacy request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LinkaMate develops or as legal and operational requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
